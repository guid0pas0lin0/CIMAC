PPWR: check your packaging compliance

Regulation (EU) 2025/40, known as the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), introduces new requirements for packaging and packaging waste. Reviewing the documentation you already have is a practical first step towards identifying compliance gaps.

Is your name or brand shown on the packaging?

Depending on your role under the PPWR, you may be considered the manufacturer even when another company physically produces the packaging. Check your actual responsibilities and the requirements applicable to your products.

01 — Technical documentation

The technical file forms the basis for demonstrating packaging compliance. Annex VII sets out the relevant documentation requirements.

02 — Laboratory testing

The necessary checks depend on packaging materials and intended use. They may cover heavy metals and, where relevant, PFAS restrictions for food-contact packaging.

03 — EU declaration of conformity

The declaration must follow applicable requirements and the model established in Annex VIII of the PPWR.

Is your documentation complete?

CIMAC can review existing documentation, identify missing elements, support completion of the technical file, determine relevant laboratory tests and assist with preparing the EU declaration of conformity.
