PPE conformity assessment: are you following the correct procedure?

Regulation (EU) 2016/425 sets out conformity assessment procedures according to the risks against which PPE is designed to protect. Correctly identifying the PPE category is a crucial first step for manufacturers.

Start with the PPE category

Identify the risks addressed by the product and determine whether it falls within Category I, II or III.

Testing and technical documentation

Identify applicable essential health and safety requirements and harmonised standards, plan the necessary testing and compile the technical documentation.

When a notified body is required

Category II PPE requires EU type-examination by a notified body followed by internal production control. Category III PPE also requires ongoing conformity assessment of production under Module C2 or Module D, as applicable.

A clear route to CE marking

Completing the appropriate procedure enables the manufacturer to draw up the EU declaration of conformity and affix the CE marking. CIMAC can help clarify the technical steps within its scope.
