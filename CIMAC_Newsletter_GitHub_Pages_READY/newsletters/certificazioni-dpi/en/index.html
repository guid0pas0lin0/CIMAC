PPE certification: are you following the right procedure?

The EU PPE Regulation provides different conformity assessment procedures depending on the level of risk a product is designed to address. Getting the classification right is essential for manufacturers.

Start with the PPE category

Identify the hazards the product protects against and determine whether Category I, II or III applies.

Tests and technical files

Review applicable requirements and technical standards, plan the necessary product tests, and prepare the supporting technical documentation.

When a notified body is required

For Category II and III PPE, conformity assessment includes the relevant involvement of a notified body, with additional production surveillance requirements for Category III products.

A clear route to CE marking

Following the correct procedure supports the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking. CIMAC can help clarify the technical steps within its scope.
