Safety footwear: which CE conformity assessment procedure applies?

Safety footwear designed to protect against occupational hazards falls within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2016/425 on personal protective equipment (PPE). Before placing footwear on the EU market, manufacturers must identify the applicable essential health and safety requirements and follow the appropriate conformity assessment procedure.

Identify the applicable requirements

Define the footwear's intended use and protective functions, identify the relevant risks and PPE category, and determine the applicable standards and test requirements.

Testing and technical documentation

Performance testing and a complete technical file provide the evidence needed to support conformity with the applicable requirements.

Conformity assessment and CE marking

The conformity assessment route depends on the PPE category. For Category II and III PPE, an EU notified body is involved in the applicable assessment procedure. The manufacturer must draw up the EU declaration of conformity and affix the CE marking once the relevant requirements have been met.
