Safety footwear: when is CE certification required?

Safety footwear intended to protect against occupational risks falls within the EU personal protective equipment framework. Before placing products on the EU market, manufacturers must identify the relevant requirements and conformity assessment procedures.

Identify the applicable requirements

Define intended use and hazards, determine the relevant PPE category and evaluate the technical standards and tests applicable to the footwear.

Testing and technical documentation

Appropriate laboratory testing, design assessments and documentation help demonstrate product performance and compliance.

Conformity assessment and CE marking

The conformity assessment route depends on the PPE category. Where required, an EU notified body participates in the assessment before the manufacturer draws up the EU declaration of conformity and affixes the CE marking.
